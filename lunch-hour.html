Lunch Hour

Tuesdays & Fridays

The Union gathers in the middle of the day for worship, testimonies and the teaching of the word.

Midday on the hill

Lunch Hour is the Union’s gathering in the middle of the academic day — worship, testimonies from members, and the ministry of the word. Visitors are welcomed at every meeting.

Guest ministers join the Union through the year. Recordings of past meetings are published on the Union’s YouTube channel.
