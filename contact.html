Visiting

  • Come to Lunch Hour on a Tuesday or Friday and introduce yourself — visitors are welcomed at every gathering.
  • If you tell us your hall, college or hostel, we will connect you to the fellowship that meets nearest to you.
  • The prayer altar is open to any student who wants to pray, whether or not you have ever attended a fellowship before.
