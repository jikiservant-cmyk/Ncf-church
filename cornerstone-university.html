Cornerstone University

Cornerstone University

Mission:

Cornerstone University exists to equip critical and creative servant leaders who will employ a Christian worldview for community transformation in all facets of life.

Ownership:

Proposed Cornerstone Christian University is owned by Pastors Discipleship Network (PDN) Ltd.

Our Why:

There is a scholarly crisis in the African church and society today. Most African pastors are untrained, and the effects of their teaching and misuse of power are being felt in people’s lives. Moreover, many in other professions are also untrained and so the need for higher quality education for Africa has never been more dire. Africa needs a new and improved type of higher education that produces truly transformed and qualified workers.

It is the goal of Cornerstone University to train and equip the highest caliber of Pastors and leaders in various facets of society, to maximize the redemptive and restorative assignment of the church.

This way, Cornerstone University exists for the public good, the advancement of the Kingdom.

Programs

Cornerstone University will offer programs not only in theology and biblical studies, but in traditional fields like art, science, business, and education. Businessmen and women, pastors, educators, and theologians will soon be making their way into the workforce trained and equipped by experts with a Christian background.

We believe this vision will inspire a new standard for design, architecture, and learning for universities in Africa. We see this vision as a mark of progress for the church in Africa and a gateway to better community formation throughout the continent. We also see many African universities being inspired, and making changes to better their students’ experience.
