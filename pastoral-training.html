Pastoral Training

Pastoral Training

Addressing A Desperate Need For Bible Education

Over 85% of pastors in East Africa have not had access to theological training. Many pastors have expressed to us a desperate need for Bible education, while others do not feel they need training because they have made it thus far without it.

PDN currently focuses on both sets of pastors;

  • Those who feel stuck in their ministry. While they are passionate to divide God’s Word accurately, they are not fully equipped to do so.
  • Pastors who feel they don’t need further training. This perspective has led to a dangerous attitude and mind-set and is destroying the church.

Our theological training comes in three convenient packages:

01

Seminary - Lite Courses

12 weeks

02

Rooted In The Word Pastors Conferences

3 days

03

Inductive Bible Study Classes

Three levels

These programs provide the pastors with both theology training and practical pastoral skills. Through programs like our “Rooted in the Word” conferences, church leaders are empowered to discover and apply Christ-centered principles. PDN is excited to witness the growth in church performance due to the correct handling and communication of God’s Word.

These programs also help pastors live a balanced and fruitful life leading to healthy families and congregations.

Currently, over 4,800 pastors are benefiting from our training programs and we hope that in the next five years, we will be serving 30,000 churches across East Africa.
