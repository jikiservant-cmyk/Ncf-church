Leadership

Serving the fellowship

How the Union is led

The Union is led by an elected student executive, supported by fellowship coordinators and by the University chaplaincy. Office bearers serve for a defined term and are accountable to the membership.

Chairperson

Carries the vision of the Union, chairs the executive and represents MUK CU within the University and to FOCUS-Uganda.

Vice Chairperson

Deputises for the Chairperson and oversees the coordination of the fellowships.

General Secretary

Keeps the records, correspondence and calendar of the Union, and serves the executive and membership.

Treasurer

Stewards the Union’s giving and reporting, keeping the fellowship’s finances in order and transparent.

Discipleship & Bible Study

Oversees follow-up of new believers, Bible study material and the training of fellowship leaders.

Evangelism & Missions

Coordinates campus outreach and the recess mission teams that travel beyond Kampala.

Praise & Worship

Leads the choir and praise team, and carries the worship life of the Union’s gatherings.

Prayer

Coordinates the prayer altar and intercession for the University.

Publicity & Media

Carries the Union’s announcements, recordings and publications — the Union has long published its own newsletter.

Welfare & Hospitality

Cares for members’ practical needs and welcomes visitors and guest ministers.

Chaplaincy

The Union works alongside the University chaplaincy at St Francis Chapel, which serves students and staff of all denominations. Rev. Canon Onesimus Asiimwe has served as Chaplain of St Francis Chapel.

Office bearers serve the fellowship; they do not sit above it.
