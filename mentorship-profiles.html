Mentorship Profiles

Mentorship Profiles

One of MUK CU’s main objectives is to establish and foster mentoring relationships between pastors in the West and in Africa. In this program, trained pastors in the West are connected to a pastor in Africa. Both pastors commit to follow MUK CU’s one year syllabus, after which the African pastor is commissioned to mentor a fellow African pastor who has no ability to communicate with a mentor in the West.

Tony Rovis Wafula

Pastor of Springs of Life Baptist Church with a congregation of 30 members.

Mission: We are committed to building & developing an intimate relationship with God and the community.

I am married with one child. I am currently pursuing a masters degree in Business Administration and have a degree in Education. We have just started the ministry and with the growth we are experiencing, this mentor relationship opportunity comes at the right time – Praise God!

Livingston Mugabbi Kayumba

Senior Pastor of Glory Revival Church that has a congregation of 250 members.

Mission: To reach out to the communities and nations by sharing the love of Jesus and giving them hope through the Gospel and making Disciples.

I am married to 1 wife, Joyce, and have 4 children Rebecca, Shibah, Comfort and Joel. I have a diploma in theology and hope this mentor relationship can help me grow in faith, ministry and equip me with skill to mentor others who don’t have such an opportunity due to language barrier and poverty. God bless you.

George Otaka

The senior pastor of Truth Evangelistic Fountain Church with a congregation of 145 members.

Mission: To set people free by preaching the Word of Truth. (John 8:32)

I am a water engineer, hydro geologist and a pastor without theological training. I am married with 4 children. I love reading and thank MUK CU for the plans to put us for us as pastors a resource center and this mentorship relationship opportunity. I hope to grow and serve better through this mentorship relationship.

Sam Mayambala Ssubyakusaaga

Senior Pastor of Healing Power Center Church with 104 members.

Mission: To bring people to Christ and teaching them the Word of God such that they are healed spiritually and physically.

I have been married for 12 years and been in ministry for 15 years. I have no training, but by God’s grace I have led these people this far. This mentor relationship will not only help me, but the whole church. Thank you, MUK CU, for training me, giving me a Bible and now giving me this opportunity. God bless you.
