News

From the hill

March 2025

Night of Redemption fills the Sports Ground

The Makerere Night of Redemption, organised by the Makerere University Christian Union, was reported by the University as a big success. Pastor Robert Kayanja was the chief celebrant at the University’s Sports Ground.

Before the event the Vice Chancellor hosted Pastor Kayanja at his office, where they discussed opportunities for collaboration between Makerere University and Robert Kayanja Ministries in the improvement of student welfare. He also addressed the Top Management of the University.

For the Union the night is the harvest of a year of prayer in the fellowships and at the altar — and an open door for students who would never walk into a meeting on their own.

November 2022

Sixty years on the hill

Makerere University Christian Union — a fellowship under FOCUS — marked sixty years since its inception.

The Union was founded in 1962 by students including Daniel Kyanda and Michael Ssenyimba. It sparked the Christian Union movement in Uganda, leading to the formation of CUs in other universities and institutions, and ultimately to FOCUS-Uganda in 1972.

Ten years earlier the Union had gathered alumni at Freedom Square for its Golden Jubilee — a day of fellowship, testimony and re-union, thanking God for the countless lives changed through the CU.

Reflection

Why the fellowship still matters

A university can be a lonely place. A student can sit in a lecture of three hundred and be known by no one. The fellowship exists so that this is not the whole story.

In the hall, college and hostel fellowships a student is known by name, prayed for by name, and helped to hold on to faith through the pressure of the academic year.

That is why the Union keeps its rhythm — Lunch Hour in the middle of the day, the prayer altar in the evening, and the small gatherings through the week. God’s saving grace is abundant to reach every soul on campus.
