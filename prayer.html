Prayer Altar

Where we get our strength

Intercession for the University

The prayer altar is where the Union draws its strength. Members gather in the evenings to intercede for the University, for one another, for the nation and for the lost on campus.

Prayer is not treated as a programme among programmes. It undergirds the fellowships, the outreach and every large gathering the Union holds.

When we gather

Main CU Prayer Altar

Thursdays · 6:00 PM

Evening intercession at St Francis Chapel — where the Union draws its strength and its wisdom.
