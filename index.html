Who We Are

An interdenominational fellowship of Christian students at Makerere University.

Makerere University Christian Union is an interdenominational fellowship of Christian students at Makerere University, Kampala. We exist to preach Christ and to minister God’s love on campus and beyond — gathering believers from every hall of residence, college and hostel into one family of faith.

“Preaching Christ with Love”

— This Year’s Theme · Ephesians 4:14–16

“Preaching Christ and ministering God’s love on campus and beyond.”

— Our Mission

The Union is not a church and does not replace one. Members are encouraged to stay rooted in their own congregations while finding, on campus, a community that studies the Scriptures, prays together and carries the gospel to fellow students.

Life in the Union moves through the fellowships. A student belongs first to the fellowship of their hall, college or hostel, where they are known by name; the wider Union gathers for Lunch Hour, the prayer altar, missions and the big campus events that mark the academic year.

MUK CU is affiliated to the Fellowship of Christian Unions (FOCUS-Uganda), the body that links Christian Unions across Ugandan universities and institutions.

“A body that brings together all believers in Makerere through its Hall, College and Hostel Fellowships — preaching Christ and ministering God’s love on campus and beyond.”

— Our Vision

“God’s saving grace is abundant to reach every soul on campus.”

Our Story

On the hill since 1962

Six decades on the hill

Makerere University Christian Union was started in 1962 by students including Daniel Kyanda and Michael Ssenyimba, who later became Bishop Ssenyimba and Vice-Chancellor of Ndejje University.

What began as a student fellowship on the Makerere hill sparked the Christian Union movement in Uganda. Christian Unions were formed in other universities and institutions across the country, and in 1972 those fellowships came together as the Fellowship of Christian Unions (FOCUS-Uganda).

The Union marked fifty years in November 2012 with a jubilee celebration at Freedom Square on the main campus, gathering alumni and students to give thanks for the lives changed through the CU. Sixty years were celebrated in November 2022.

More than six decades on, the calling has not changed: to reach every soul on campus with the saving grace of God.

1962The Christian Union is founded at Makerere by Daniel Kyanda, Michael Ssenyimba and fellow students.
1972The movement begun on the hill leads to the formation of FOCUS-Uganda, linking Christian Unions nationwide.
2012The Union celebrates its Golden Jubilee — 50 years — at Freedom Square with alumni and students.
2022Sixty years of the Union’s witness on campus are marked with a thanksgiving celebration.
TodayHall, College and Hostel Fellowships carry the work forward across every part of the University.

Hall Fellowships

Gatherings within the University halls of residence — among them Nkrumah, Nsibirwa, Livingstone, University Hall, Mitchell, Africa, Mary Stuart and the CCE Complex.

Together they number about 89 students, averaging around 17 per fellowship.

Hall fellowships meet through the week for Bible study, prayer and worship, and are the first place a resident student is welcomed.

College Fellowships

Eleven fellowships meet around the colleges and schools of the University, so that students gather with those who share their timetable and discipline.

They include Conas, FSTNB, SSP, COCIS, MESCA, COBAMS, DASE, CHUSS, SOL, SFEGS and SFTNB — about 485 students in all, averaging around 44.

Most meet at noon or during lunch hour; SOL gathers on Fridays.

Hostel Fellowships

Six fellowships take the Union to students living in hostels around the University: Paramount, Garden Courts, Kare, Mish, Baskon and New Bush Zion.

Together they number about 150 students, averaging around 25 — so that distance from campus is no barrier to belonging.

Mission Team Fellowships

Ten mission teams carry the work beyond the hill, among them Kumi Team, Jesu Is King, Jesus is Lord Ministry, Tororo Busia, Teso (TEMISS), MUSACRIF, Campus Chapter, RUF, Lango Gospel Missioners and the Mbale–Kapchorwa–Sironko team.

About 252 students serve in these teams, averaging around 25 each.

Affiliations

We do not walk alone

FOCUS-Uganda

The Fellowship of Christian Unions links Christian Unions in universities and institutions across Uganda. It was formed in 1972 out of the movement that began at Makerere.

MUK CU is a fellowship under FOCUS, sharing in its training, missions and national gatherings.

Makerere University Chaplaincy

St Francis Chapel serves the spiritual life of the University community. The Union works alongside the chaplaincy in ministering to students.

Alumni of the Union

Six decades of graduates carry the Union’s witness into law, medicine, engineering, education, business and public service.

Alumni return for jubilee gatherings, mentor current students and support the work financially.
