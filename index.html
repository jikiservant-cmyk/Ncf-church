What We Do at MSU
Scripture Union at Makerere is part of Scripture Union Uganda — a movement committed to children and youth following Jesus, filled with hope, and transforming families and nations.
  • 📖 Weekly Bible studies & discipleship
  • 🙏 Prayer & intercession for the campus
  • 🎶 Worship & praise fellowships
  • 🤝 Evangelism & outreach to students
  • 🏕️ Retreats, camps & conferences
  • 💡 Leadership & mentorship training
