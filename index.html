What We Do at MUCU
Makerere University Christian Union brings together all believers in Makerere through its Hall, College and Hostel Fellowships. Founded in 1962, MUCU is part of the Fellowship of Christian Unions (FOCUS-Uganda).
  • 📖 Weekly Bible studies & discipleship
  • 🙏 Prayer & intercession for the campus
  • 🎶 Worship & praise fellowships
  • 🤝 Evangelism & outreach to students
  • 🏕️ Retreats, camps & conferences
  • 💡 Leadership & mentorship training
