Who We Are

Makerere University Christian Union (MUCU / MukCU) is a student-led Christian fellowship based at Makerere University on Makerere Hill, Kampala, Uganda. We exist to preach Christ and minister God's love to every soul on campus.

Founded in 1962 by Daniel Kyanda and Bishop Micheal Ssenyimba, MUCU sparked off the Christian Union movement in Uganda — leading to C.Us across the country and the formation of FOCUS-Uganda in 1972. MUCU remains a proud member of FOCUS-Uganda.
