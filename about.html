Our Story
A Christ-centred student fellowship at Makerere University, founded in 1962.

Makerere University Christian Union (MUCU / MukCU) is a student-led Christian fellowship based at Makerere University on Makerere Hill, Kampala, Uganda. We exist to preach Christ and minister God's love to every soul on campus.

MUCU was founded in 1962 by the likes of Daniel Kyanda and Bishop Micheal Ssenyimba (now retired Vice-Chancellor of Ndejje University). It sparked off the Christian Union movement in Uganda, leading to the formation of C.Us in other universities and institutions across the country, and ultimately the Fellowship of Christian Unions (FOCUS-Uganda) in 1972. MUCU remains a proud member of FOCUS-Uganda.

MUCU brings together all believers in Makerere through its Hall, College and Hostel Fellowships. It is the largest fellowship at the University, with a membership of over 2,000 students. Ministries such as Jesus is King Ministry operate under the MUCU umbrella, and flagship events like the Makerere Night of Redemption are organised by the Union.
