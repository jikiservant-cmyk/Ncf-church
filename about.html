Makerere University Scripture Union — growing disciples on the Ivory Tower.

Our Story
A Christ-centred student fellowship at Makerere University, affiliated with Scripture Union Uganda.

Makerere University Scripture Union (MSU) is a student-led Christian fellowship based at Makerere University on Makerere Hill, Kampala, Uganda. We exist to help students know Jesus Christ deeply, grow in their faith, and live as bold witnesses for Him on campus and beyond.

We are affiliated with Scripture Union Uganda, a movement whose vision is: "Children and Youth, following Jesus, filled with hope and transforming the families and nations of Africa." Through Scripture Union Uganda, Makerere students connect to a wider network of school and university fellowships across the country.

Our ministry on campus centres around the Word of God, prayer, worship, discipleship, evangelism, and service. Whether you are a first-year student finding your feet or a final-year student preparing for life after campus, there is a place for you here.

Our Vision & Mission

Vision: Students following Jesus, transforming Makerere University and the nation.

Mission: To reach students with the gospel, disciple them in God's Word, and send them out to serve Christ in every sphere of life.

Core values: Christ-centredness • Integrity • Servant leadership • Unity in diversity • Excellence
